# Reward-data-quality feasibility study

## Status

The former token-length-tilt pilot remains closed. This is a separate,
offline-first successor study. Four fixed-budget reward models have been
frozen using the all-data, diagnostic-filtered, global-random, and
direction-matched-earlier-year conditions. Their common-development metrics
were recorded before any held-out human quality outcome was used.

## Research question

Does exclusion of DA dataset slices identified by the frozen AmanaMT
diagnostic improve reward reliability under increasing best-of-N selection
pressure, beyond the effect of removing a comparable amount of training data?

The intervention is **dataset-slice exclusion**. It is not evidence that
individual annotations inside an excluded slice are incorrect.

## Conditions

All reward models must share architecture, initialization procedure, training
budget, optimizer, score calibration, and development decisions.

1. All eligible DA slices.
2. Frozen diagnostic-flagged DA slices excluded.
3. Repeated random slice exclusions matched as closely as possible on label
   volume and available language, direction, year, protocol, and system
   composition.

Any unmatched strata are a limitation and part of the estimand. An MQM-trained
model may be added later as a comparator, but it is not an upper bound.

## Holdout policy

WMT25 MQM is the primary offline endpoint because its released candidate pools
are rectangular and directly match the diagnostic's DA-versus-MQM motivation.
WMT26 cESA is a secondary external check. Its Simplified-Chinese-to-Japanese
direction has complete retained-evidence coverage under the coverage audit;
other WMT26 directions require separate missingness handling and are not part
of the frozen secondary endpoint.

During design, scripts may inspect only identifiers, candidate names,
assignment coverage, archive hashes, schema, and whether a cESA score field is
present and structurally valid. They must not retain or summarize MQM errors,
cESA score values, preferences, error spans, or derived quality outcomes.
Coverage statistics and hashes are generated by
`src/audit_reward_holdouts.py` and merged into `results/paper_stats.json`.

WMT25 and WMT26 must not determine diagnostic thresholds, exclusion choices,
reward-model hyperparameters, checkpoint selection, or stopping rules.

## Frozen slice diagnostic

The exclusion unit is a language-pair-by-year DA slice. D2 is Spearman
agreement between COMET and BLEURT minus Spearman agreement between COMET and
DA on the same deterministic slice sample. The diagnostic sample contains at
most 1,000 rows per slice and uses seed 20261031.

A slice is flagged only when the lower endpoint of its paired 95% bootstrap
interval for D2 exceeds 0.50. An interval crossing the threshold is
inconclusive and the slice remains in the targeted condition. The threshold
was selected from the already-completed AmanaMT discovery analysis before
scoring the broader DA inventory. It must not be changed after broad scoring.

COMET and BLEURT overlap historically with parts of WMT DA and therefore do not
provide independent evidence that a label is wrong. They define the frozen
data-selection procedure; the untouched human holdouts test whether that
procedure is useful.

The source dataset has no system identifier. Matched-random controls can match
label volume, year, direction, domain distribution, annotator-count
distribution, and sometimes language pair across years, but cannot match
system composition. Both a year/direction/volume match and a language-pair
across-year sensitivity match should be reported where feasible.

## Offline estimand

For each frozen reward model and each prespecified nested candidate pool,
select the highest predicted-reward translation. Compare its held-out human
quality with the selections made by the all-data model and matched-random
controls. The primary comparison is the change in selected human quality as
the candidate pool grows.

Candidate nesting must be fixed independently of reward and human outcomes.
Repeated pool orders must be shared across reward models. Analyses must remain
paired within the same source and candidate pool. WMT26 analyses may use only
prespecified complete-coverage subsets or an explicitly justified estimator
for its dynamic missingness; it must not silently treat unjudged candidates as
low quality.

For WMT25, the frozen nested candidate counts are 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and the full
candidate pool. One hundred deterministic candidate permutations per source
are generated from seed 20261103 and shared across reward models. Human MQM is
computed with the official WMT25 severity rule: major errors cost 5 points,
minor punctuation errors cost 0.1, other minor errors cost 1, and a
nontranslation receives the fixed score -25. These choices were frozen before
summarizing any held-out quality scores.

The WMT26 external check uses the same nested candidate counts through 16 and
then the full Chinese-to-Japanese candidate pool, with the same deterministic
permutation count, permutation seed, paired segment analysis, and cluster
bootstrap resampling rule. The released `Human (postediting)` output is excluded
before candidate permutations are built because it is an evaluation artifact,
not a deployable MT-system action. Its retained mean cESA score is the human
endpoint. This protocol-transfer result will not be used to retune the
diagnostic or reward models.

## Launch boundary

This phase supports a claim about offline reward optimization, not policy
gradient training. A later REINFORCE extension is conditional on useful
offline evidence, a separately frozen protocol, and an evaluation plan for new
translations. Existing WMT judgments cannot evaluate newly generated outputs.

## Next implementation gate

Before reward training, produce a DA slice inventory containing provenance,
language direction, year, evaluation protocol, system composition, label
count, AmanaMT diagnostic status, and prior use in diagnostic development.
Then determine whether matched random slice exclusions are identifiable with
the available number and composition of slices.
